# MySQL Introduction

This repository contains 15 exercises on SQL tables. Below is the list of files and their content:

- [01-products.sql](./01-products.sql): Management of products for an online store (e.g. name, price, quantity, etc.).
- [02-shipments.sql](./02-shipments.sql): Management of shipping details for an order (e.g. sender, recipient, weight, volume, date, etc.).
- [03-students.sql](./03-students.sql): Management of student records (e.g. name, student ID, email, etc.).
- [04-inventory.sql](./04-inventory.sql): Management of library inventory (e.g. title, author, publisher, ISBN, etc.).
- [05-properties.sql](./05-properties.sql): Management of real estate properties (e.g. address, property type, square meters, rooms, price, etc.).
- [06-projects.sql](./06-projects.sql): Management of internal projects (e.g. name, allocated budget, start date, end date, etc.).
- [07-vehicles.sql](./07-vehicles.sql): Management of dealership vehicles (e.g. brand, model, price, mileage, license plate, etc.).
- [08-flights.sql](./08-flights.sql): Management of flight bookings (e.g. origin, destination, flight number, airline, duration, etc.).
- [09-tickets.sql](./09-tickets.sql): Management of tech support requests (e.g. ticket number, username, request, status, etc.).
- [10-reservations.sql](./10-reservations.sql): Management of restaurant reservations (e.g. customer name, date and time, number of guests, etc.).
- [11-jobs.sql](./11-jobs.sql): Management of job offers (e.g. title, position, salary, contract type, etc.).
- [12-invoices.sql](./12-invoices.sql): Management of issued invoices (e.g. invoice number, customer, total, issue date, etc.).
- [13-leaves.sql](./13-leaves.sql): Management of staff leave requests (e.g. requester name, request type, start date, end date, approved status, etc.).
- [14-reviews.sql](./14-reviews.sql): Management of customer reviews (e.g. customer, product, rating, comment, review date, etc.).
- [15-donations.sql](./15-donations.sql): Management of donations to an association (e.g. donor name, amount, donation date, reason, etc.).
